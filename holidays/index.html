Tue Nov 10 – Thu Dec 17 · Gifts back to Guest Services Mon-Fri 9 AM-5 PM

Redmond Town Center Giving Tree

Pick a tag from the tree, buy the requested gift for a local young person and return it unwrapped by Dec 17.

Center Court (tags) and RTC Management Office, Redmond Town Center · Price of the gift you buy · Organizer's page

Thu Nov 26 – Fri Jan 1 · Thanksgiving, Christmas Day, New Year's Day

Sound Transit holiday schedules

Link and ST Express run Sunday schedules on Thanksgiving, Christmas Day and New Year's Day; on Nov 27 Link runs a Saturday schedule and ST Express a weekday one.

Link light rail and ST Express · Regular fares · Organizer's page

Thu Nov 26 · All day

King County transfer stations closed on Thanksgiving

King County offices and its transfer stations and recycling facilities close on Thanksgiving Day; garbage pickup changes come from your own hauler.

King County solid waste facilities, including the Factoria Recycling and Transfer Station · Organizer's page

Fri Nov 27 – Thu Dec 24 · Daily; reservations recommended

Santa Photos at Redmond Town Center

Santa takes photo visits through Christmas Eve; pets come on Mondays Dec 7, 14 and 21.

Between Temaki and Fuego, Redmond Town Center · Photos for purchase · Organizer's page

Wed Dec 2 · Time on the registration page

Kids Light Fashion Show Workshop

Redmond library staff help kids and families build lit-up outfits for the Redmond Lights fashion show.

Redmond Senior & Community Center · Free; registration required · Organizer's page

Sat Dec 5 · 4 to 8 PM

Redmond Lights Kick-Off

Four stages of music and dance, snowfall every 15 minutes, a silent disco, free crafts, the Kids Light Fashion Show at 6 PM and a family lights promenade at 6:30.

Downtown Park · Free · Organizer's page

Sat Dec 5 – Mon Jan 4 · Evenings; nightly hours not posted

Redmond Lights art and light installations

Large lit artworks stay up in both parks for a month, with buskers at Esterra Park on December Saturdays.

Downtown Park and Esterra Park · Free · Organizer's page
