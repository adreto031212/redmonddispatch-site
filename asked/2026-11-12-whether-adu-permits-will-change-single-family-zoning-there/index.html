Reader asked

Whether ADU permits will change single-family zoning there

November 12, 2026 · Redmond Dispatch, issue 6 · Every answered question

A reader on Education Hill asked whether ADU permits will change single-family zoning there. The zoning has already changed: as of January 1, 2025, Redmond folded its former single-family zones, R-1 through R-8 and RIN, into one Neighborhood Residential zone, and the city says middle housing such as multiplexes, stacked flats and cottages is broadly allowed in residential areas, consistent with state law. Accessory dwelling units follow state law too. RCW 36.70A.681 requires cities to allow at least two ADUs, attached or detached, on lots in zones that allow single-family homes, and bars a rule that the owner live on the lot. An ADU permit is reviewed under the zone already in place; it does not rezone anything. To see your own lot's zone, use the city's interactive map at gis.redmond.gov/pv, or call the Planner on Call at 425-556-2494. City of Redmond · RCW 36.70A.681
