What the city decided and what changed

Drone delivery hubs get a public hearing at the Planning Commission on Wednesday, November 18

Since April the city has barred drone ports, and smaller commercial drone uses, in every zone while it writes permanent rules.

On the calendar

Redmond Town Center Giving Tree

Nov 10 to Dec 17 · Gifts back to Guest Services Mon-Fri 9 AM-5 PM · Center Court (tags) and RTC Management Office, Redmond Town Center. Pick a tag from the tree, buy the requested gift for a local young person and return it unwrapped by Dec 17. Organizer's page

Sound Transit holiday schedules

Nov 26 to Jan 1 · Thanksgiving, Christmas Day, New Year's Day · Link light rail and ST Express. Link and ST Express run Sunday schedules on Thanksgiving, Christmas Day and New Year's Day; on Nov 27 Link runs a Saturday schedule and ST Express a weekday one. Organizer's page

King County transfer stations closed on Thanksgiving

Nov 26 · All day · King County solid waste facilities, including the Factoria Recycling and Transfer Station. King County offices and its transfer stations and recycling facilities close on Thanksgiving Day; garbage pickup changes come from your own hauler. Organizer's page

Santa Photos at Redmond Town Center

Nov 27 to Dec 24 · Daily; reservations recommended · Between Temaki and Fuego, Redmond Town Center. Santa takes photo visits through Christmas Eve; pets come on Mondays Dec 7, 14 and 21. Organizer's page
