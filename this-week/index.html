Redmond · Nov 26 to Dec 2

This week in Redmond.

The dated things to know between now and next Thursday, from issue 8 of the Redmond Dispatch. Every item links its public source.

What the city decided and what changed

Two Redmond council seats open in January, and applications open Monday

Councilmember Jessica Forsythe, elected to the state House for the 48th District, plans to resign Position 3 effective January 5, joining Vanessa Kritzer's Position 5.

NE 128th Way reopened November 18, two days ahead of the county's date

King County crews opened the road just west of Avondale Road NE that Wednesday with the new culvert in place.

The 2027 fees on new homes are set

The council adopted them on its November 17 consent agenda.

Redmond is writing a new accessibility plan for its streets, its first since 2007

The plan covers the public right-of-way: it adds new data to a 2025 assessment, names specific barriers and ranks them for removal, with outreach to the disability community.

the leftovers go in the gray cart

If your garbage day is Thursday or Friday, pickup comes a day later this week: Thursday's on Friday, Friday's on Saturday.

Who decides when and where Redmond's roads can close

A reader asked who decides when and where Redmond's roads can close.

On the calendar

Redmond Town Center Giving Tree

Nov 10 to Dec 17 · Gifts back to Guest Services Mon-Fri 9 AM-5 PM · Center Court (tags) and RTC Management Office, Redmond Town Center. Pick a tag from the tree, buy the requested gift for a local young person and return it unwrapped by Dec 17. Organizer's page

Sound Transit holiday schedules

Nov 26 to Jan 1 · Thanksgiving, Christmas Day, New Year's Day · Link light rail and ST Express. Link and ST Express run Sunday schedules on Thanksgiving, Christmas Day and New Year's Day; on Nov 27 Link runs a Saturday schedule and ST Express a weekday one. Organizer's page

King County transfer stations closed on Thanksgiving

Nov 26 · All day · King County solid waste facilities, including the Factoria Recycling and Transfer Station. King County offices and its transfer stations and recycling facilities close on Thanksgiving Day; garbage pickup changes come from your own hauler. Organizer's page

Santa Photos at Redmond Town Center

Nov 27 to Dec 24 · Daily; reservations recommended · Between Temaki and Fuego, Redmond Town Center. Santa takes photo visits through Christmas Eve; pets come on Mondays Dec 7, 14 and 21. Organizer's page

Kids Light Fashion Show Workshop

Dec 2 · Time on the registration page · Redmond Senior & Community Center. Redmond library staff help kids and families build lit-up outfits for the Redmond Lights fashion show. Organizer's page

Redmond Lights Kick-Off

Dec 5 · 4 to 8 PM · Downtown Park. Four stages of music and dance, snowfall every 15 minutes, a silent disco, free crafts, the Kids Light Fashion Show at 6 PM and a family lights promenade at 6:30. Organizer's page

Redmond Lights art and light installations

Dec 5 to Jan 4 · Evenings; nightly hours not posted · Downtown Park and Esterra Park. Large lit artworks stay up in both parks for a month, with buskers at Esterra Park on December Saturdays. Organizer's page
