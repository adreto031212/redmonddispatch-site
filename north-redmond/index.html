The latest in North Redmond

November 12, 2026

The gate on 172nd Avenue NE at NE 124th Street is set to come out, with traffic circles and streetlights at NE 124th Street and NE 126th Place and two speed cushions in between. King County has approved the permits for the stretch north of the city limit. The city's page says construction is anticipated in 2026 with the exact schedule still to be set, and barricades stand in for the gate until then. City of Redmond

November 5, 2026

Smith Woods is a ten-acre park in planning, just outside the city limit along NE 124th Street, east of 176th Avenue NE. The plan includes a picnic area, an overlook pavilion, a stream crossing and natural play. No construction date is set, and a possible levy lid lift could fund a first phase. City of Redmond

October 29, 2026

Just past the city line, King County now expects NE 128th Way, closed just west of Avondale Road NE for a fish-passage culvert, to reopen November 20 instead of October 30. Crews found the job bigger than planned and are doing nearby work during the same closure so the road does not have to close again later; much of what remains is concrete paving that has to cure. The signed detour, just over five miles, uses SR 202, NE 116th Street and Avondale Road NE. King County

October 22, 2026

Smith Woods, east of 176th Avenue NE between NE 122nd and NE 124th Streets, is ten acres the city bought in two parts, in 2000 and 2011. A trail and natural play and art elements went in after the 2009–2010 master plan. Saturday, October 31, it hosts Green Redmond Day's native planting, 9 AM to noon. Smith Woods · Green Redmond Day

October 15, 2026

NE 124th Street began as a cow path and was a developed road by 1964, and one of the neighborhood's original 1909 homesteads still stands. The neighborhood also has access to the Puget Power Trail, a 3.1-mile soft-surface route between the Sammamish River Trail and Farrel-McWhirter Park, open to hikers, mountain bikes and horses. North Redmond · Puget Power Trail

October 12, 2026

Just past the city line, King County's NE 128th Way stays closed between 181st Avenue NE and Avondale Road NE for a fish-passage culvert; the county extended the closure for drainage work and now expects to reopen the road October 30. The detour, just over five miles, uses SR 202, NE 116th Street and Avondale Road NE. King County

Questions people ask

Is this a real estate newsletter?

No. It is a Redmond newsletter that happens to know the housing market. Three of the four sections are about living here. The fourth tells you what homes are selling for, because in Redmond that is news too.

Who writes it?

Neighborhood Press LLC, an independent publisher. Every item comes from Redmond's own public record (council agendas, permit notices, school district and transit updates, parks calendars) and links its source. It is researched and written with AI tools, and an editor reads every issue before it goes out.

How often, and is it really free?

Once a week, Thursday at 6 AM, about five minutes to read. Free, no paywall, unsubscribe in one click at the bottom of any issue.

Who owns it?

Neighborhood Press LLC, an independent publishing company. The newsletter is free, and no sponsor buys coverage. Our standards.

What happens with my address?

It is used for one thing: a monthly estimate of what your home would sell for today. It goes to a local real estate brokerage under the same ownership as our publisher only if you tick the sharing box on the form; that brokerage may then contact you about your home. Leave the box empty and it stays with us. Never sold or posted. Remove it any time by replying to any issue.
