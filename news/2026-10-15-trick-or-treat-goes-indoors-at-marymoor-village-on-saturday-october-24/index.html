Trick-or-treat goes indoors at Marymoor Village on Saturday, October 24

October 15, 2026 · Redmond Dispatch, issue 2 · All Redmond news

From 4:30 to 7:30 PM, kids go room to room for candy, with crafts, at the Redmond Community Center at Marymoor Village, 6505 176th Avenue NE; register through the city. The same evening, 5 to 9 PM, a five-minute haunted house runs for ages 12 and up, tickets per person online or at the door. Trick or Treat · Haunted House
