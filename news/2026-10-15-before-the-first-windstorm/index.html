Redmond · One useful thing

before the first windstorm

October 15, 2026 · Redmond Dispatch, issue 2 · All Redmond news

Fall windstorms are when the power goes out. The city's guidance: keep enough food, water and supplies for a few days, ideally two weeks, with one gallon of water per person per day. Sign up for AlertRedmond, the city's emergency notification system, and in a widespread outage tune a battery radio to the city's emergency station, AM 1650, which also streams online.
