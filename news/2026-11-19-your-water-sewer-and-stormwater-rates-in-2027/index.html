Redmond · One useful thing

your water, sewer and stormwater rates in 2027

November 19, 2026 · Redmond Dispatch, issue 7 · All Redmond news

The rate ordinances on the council's November 10 budget agenda raise Redmond water rates 5% and wastewater rates 8.5% in 2027, and by the same again in 2028. King County's wastewater treatment charge, passed through on the sewer bill, goes up 12.75% in each year. The single-family stormwater base rate becomes $17.23 a month in 2027 and $17.40 in 2028. Homes in the Novelty Hill service area see 3% a year on water and on wastewater. Budget memo
