Council meets Tuesday on a new fire fee, a new permitting department and the budget

October 12, 2026 · Redmond Dispatch, issue 1 · All Redmond news

Three sessions at City Hall, 15670 NE 85th Street, on October 13. At 4:30 PM a committee gets a budget-process update and a resolution to sell the city's former Pacer Propane lot at 18459 NE 76th Street. At 6:15 PM council meets jointly with the parks commission. The 7 PM study session takes up a fire impact fee on new development of $1.03 per square foot for all home types, replacing today's per-unit fee from January 1, 2027, and a new department that would bring development review and permitting under one roof. Study sessions are discussion; the fee is slated for approval November 17. Watch on RCTV at redmond.gov/rctvlive. Committee agenda · Study session agenda · Fire fee staff report
