November 2, not October 31

October 12, 2026 · Redmond Dispatch, issue 1 · All Redmond news

The second half of 2026 King County property tax is due Monday, November 2; October 31 falls on a Saturday. Pay online, or mail a check postmarked by the due date to King County Treasury, 201 S. Jackson St., Suite 710, Seattle, WA 98104, with your account number on it. The county will email or text a reminder about 30 days ahead if you sign up. King County Treasury
