Drone delivery hubs get a public hearing at the Planning Commission on Wednesday, November 18

November 12, 2026 · Redmond Dispatch, issue 6 · All Redmond news

Since April the city has barred drone ports, and smaller commercial drone uses, in every zone while it writes permanent rules. The hearing is on the calendar as a special meeting from 7 to 9 PM, and written comments can go to the commission ahead of it. Council takes up the recommendations in February and decides by April 2027. City of Redmond · city calendar
