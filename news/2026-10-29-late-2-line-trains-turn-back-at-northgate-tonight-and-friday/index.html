Late 2 Line trains turn back at Northgate tonight and Friday

October 29, 2026 · Redmond Dispatch, issue 4 · All Redmond news

From 11 PM to the end of service on October 29 and 30, the 2 Line runs only between Downtown Redmond and Northgate while crews work on the 1 Line; riders headed to Lynnwood City Center can expect delays of up to 20 minutes. Sound Transit
