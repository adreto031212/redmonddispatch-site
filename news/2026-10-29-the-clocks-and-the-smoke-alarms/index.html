Redmond · One useful thing

the clocks and the smoke alarms

October 29, 2026 · Redmond Dispatch, issue 4 · All Redmond news

Clocks go back an hour at 2 AM on Sunday, November 1. While you reset the oven clock, press the test button on every smoke alarm. The U.S. Fire Administration says to test alarms monthly, replace the batteries in battery-powered alarms (and the backup battery in hardwired ones) at least once a year, and replace the whole alarm 10 years after the manufacture date printed on it. Alarms belong on every level of the home, inside and outside each sleeping area. U.S. Fire Administration
