Redmond's tree rules date from 1998. The first full rewrite gets a public hearing Wednesday

October 12, 2026 · Redmond Dispatch, issue 1 · All Redmond news

The Planning Commission opens its October 14 meeting, 7 PM in Council Chambers at City Hall, with testimony on the Tree Code Update. The commission returns to it November 4 and November 18; council adoption is expected in the first quarter of 2027, and comments are taken until then. Planning Commission memo · Tree Code Update
