Redmond's budget gets its final council session at 6 PM tonight

November 5, 2026 · Redmond Dispatch, issue 5 · All Redmond news

Public comment on the 2027–2028 budget came through the four hearings, and the last was November 2. The public may attend tonight's session in Council Chambers at City Hall, and the city's alert says final adoption is anticipated Tuesday, November 10. City of Redmond · budget page
