What changed in Redmond.

Every story the Redmond Dispatch has run, newest first. Each links the public record it came from.

November 12, 2026

Drone delivery hubs get a public hearing at the Planning Commission on Wednesday, November 18

Since April the city has barred drone ports, and smaller commercial drone uses, in every zone while it writes permanent rules.

The 2027–2028 budget, with water, sewer and stormwater rate increases, was up for adoption Tuesday night

The council's November 10 special meeting agenda carried the final budget, the 2027 property tax levy, a business license fee update, higher water and sewer rates, a higher monthly stormwater base…

The next council business meeting is Tuesday, November 17, at 7 PM at City Hall, 15670 NE 85th Street

The agenda was not posted as of Tuesday; it goes up at redmond.legistar.com before the meeting, and the meeting streams at redmond.gov/rctvlive.

Redmond awarded $42,000 in mini-grants to 13 youth-led climate projects

The city received 22 proposals asking for $76,000 in all.

a heat pump program closes December 14

Energy Smart Eastside's Boost program pays 100% of heat pump purchase and installation for income-qualified homeowners in Redmond, Bellevue, Issaquah, Kirkland, Mercer Island and Sammamish, and some…

November 5, 2026

Redmond's budget gets its final council session at 6 PM tonight

Public comment on the 2027–2028 budget came through the four hearings, and the last was November 2.

The Planning Commission was set to meet Wednesday, November 4, on two study sessions: commercial drone rules and the tree code update

Neither item listed a public hearing.

Redmond wants two new Planning Commission members, and the suggested deadline to apply is Friday, November 6, at 11:59 PM

The seats stay open until they are filled.

2 Line shuttle buses replace trains between South Bellevue and the International District from the start of service Saturday, November 14, through the end of service Sunday, November 15, for scheduled maintenance

Trains keep running between Downtown Redmond and South Bellevue about every 10 minutes, so a Redmond rider can reach Bellevue on the train and take the shuttle from there.

alerts in more than 200 languages

The city's AlertRedmond system sends weather and emergency notices by text, the Everbridge app, email or phone call, and its portal shows messages in over 200 languages.

October 29, 2026

Monday is the budget's last public hearing, and the property tax deadline

Council holds the fourth and final hearing on the 2027–2028 budget on Monday, November 2, at 7 PM, at City Hall and on redmond.gov/rctvlive.

Ballots are due in a drop box by 8 PM Tuesday, November 3

That is King County's Election Day cutoff; the county's drop-box page lists every location.

Late 2 Line trains turn back at Northgate tonight and Friday

From 11 PM to the end of service on October 29 and 30, the 2 Line runs only between Downtown Redmond and Northgate while crews work on the 1 Line; riders headed to Lynnwood City Center can expect…

The city's sustainability team holds its last drop-in of the year Monday

The Meet and Green runs 3 to 4:30 PM on November 2 in the Redmond Library conference room, 15990 NE 85th Street: come by any time to ask about Redmond's climate work and get a preview of what comes…

Veterans Day is Wednesday, November 11

City Hall offices close for the day, and Lake Washington School District has no school.

the clocks and the smoke alarms

Clocks go back an hour at 2 AM on Sunday, November 1.

October 22, 2026

Council starts deliberating the 2027–2028 budget tonight at 6 PM

Sessions follow on October 27 at 7 PM and October 29 and November 5 at 6 PM, at City Hall and on redmond.gov/rctvlive.

Drone delivery hubs go to the Planning Commission on Wednesday, October 28

Since April, Redmond has barred drone ports, where commercial drones are stored, charged and launched for deliveries, in all zones while it writes permanent rules.

The Evergreen Middle School rebuild is paused at 50% design for a district review

Lake Washington School District is weighing the project, planned to open in 2031, within its boundary and enrollment realignment, and says the review has no predetermined outcome.

A draft stormwater plan for the city is due out in November

It covers how Redmond moves over 11 billion gallons of rain a year through ponds and pipes to its streams, from pollution prevention to fish-barrier removal.

Trunk or Treat fills the City Hall lot on Friday, October 30

Redmond Fire hosts it from 5 to 7:30 PM at 15670 NE 85th Street, with a decorated Parks vehicle from Farrel-McWhirter.

leaves that don't fit the cart

Recology now collects Redmond's compost and yard waste.

October 15, 2026

The mayor's two-year budget gets a public hearing Tuesday, October 20

Mayor Angela Birney's preliminary 2027–2028 budget funds a rebuild of Fire Station 11, a rebuilt Teen Center, the Maintenance and Operations Center redevelopment, RedLink and a new department for…

Two new Planning Commission seats are open, with applications due November 6

The commission recommends changes to the comprehensive plan and zoning code to council, from the tree code rewrite to path projects, and meets at 7 PM on the second and fourth Wednesdays.

A free salmon walk runs on the Sammamish River Trail, October 24 and 25

From 10 AM to 2 PM both days: a walk on the salmon's journey with a scavenger hunt for prizes, and a booth at City Hall where Go Redmond hands out pre-loaded ORCA cards while supplies last.

Trick-or-treat goes indoors at Marymoor Village on Saturday, October 24

From 4:30 to 7:30 PM, kids go room to room for candy, with crafts, at the Redmond Community Center at Marymoor Village, 6505 176th Avenue NE; register through the city.

before the first windstorm

Fall windstorms are when the power goes out.

October 12, 2026

Council meets Tuesday on a new fire fee, a new permitting department and the budget

Three sessions at City Hall, 15670 NE 85th Street, on October 13.

Redmond's tree rules date from 1998. The first full rewrite gets a public hearing Wednesday

The Planning Commission opens its October 14 meeting, 7 PM in Council Chambers at City Hall, with testimony on the Tree Code Update.

No 2 Line trains west of South Bellevue this weekend

From Saturday, October 17, through the end of service Sunday, October 18, shuttle buses replace trains between South Bellevue and International District/Chinatown, every 10 to 15 minutes.

No school Friday, October 16

Lake Washington School District has a LEAP day on the 16th, and preschool programs pause October 13–15 for conferences.

Harvest Festival, Saturday, October 17, at Farrel-McWhirter Park

Two sessions, 10 AM to noon and 1 to 3 PM, at 19545 Redmond Road: pressed cider, wagon rides, butter making and a trail scavenger hunt, with a pony ride and a pumpkin for kids 12 and under.

November 2, not October 31

The second half of 2026 King County property tax is due Monday, November 2; October 31 falls on a Saturday.
